`hero_bg.py` env knobs: `SC` product width px, `BASE` foot y of the lowest item, `DX` x shift, `TABLE` x0,y0,x1,y1 inpaint+grain patch on the table, `INPAINT` `x0,y0,x1,y1;...` extra inpaint (here: removes the wall roundel so the caption sits on clean wall), `CAP_Y/CAP_SZ/CAP_DX`, `FONT`. What it does: flood-fills the white bg to get alpha, partial Reinhard colour transfer to scene, warm grade, window-light gradient, wood bounce at the base, 0.8 blur + grain, per-column foot line so bags (further back) and cans (front) each get contact shadow, cast shadow to the right and glossy reflection; caption = brush font, gold gradient, crimson outline, glow, 4 sparkles; tilt/bounce arrays are zeroed (Ray: "too crooked, just make it straighter").
